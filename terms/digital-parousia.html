Definition / 定义

定义：机器“将要降临”的期待，复制了基督再来的时间形态：已经与尚未、预兆、警醒，以及无人知晓的日子。

The expectation that the machine is "arriving," replicating the temporal shape of Christ's return: the already and the not yet, signs, vigilance, and an unknowable day.
