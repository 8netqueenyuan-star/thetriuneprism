Bring a Question

What I do

I use biblical principles to take apart questions about business and human nature, and translate them into language that can be lived with. I do not offer a single-angle answer. The same question is looked at from several dimensions — theological, human and relational, organizational, power, technical, and historical — and only then put back together.

For Scripture, I work with the BSF pattern of Observation, Interpretation, and Application. On this site, I am not preaching. I am applying that habit of multi-dimensional seeing to real confusion in the AI age.

A complete refraction — an example

Question: “AI writes in 10 minutes what takes me a day. Am I just coasting?”

Technical: AI removes the middle layer between thought and text. It shows that much of what used to be produced may never have needed “you” in the first place.

Organizational / Power: Knowledge-paying is about trust, not information. People pay because they trust that a person has sifted for them. AI can generate content; it cannot generate “I trust you.”

Human / Relational: The anxiety is not about being outpaced. It is about no longer feeling needed — an identity question: “If I no longer produce, who am I?”

Theological: Human worth was never tied to output. Creation belongs to God; human beings are stewards. When tools take over “output,” they press us back to the oldest question: what were you made for, not what were you made to produce?

Integration: Do not compete on volume. Answer this instead: in your field, what can only you give — your judgment, your experience, and the uncertainty you carry for others? That is what AI cannot take away.

My position

I am not representing any denomination. My thinking is rooted in orthodox Christian faith — imago Dei, the reality of sin, and the reality of grace. I do not believe in a “technology will save us” story, and I do not fall into end-times panic either.

I am not an AI pastor, not a ghostwriting machine, and not a counselor. I am a person doing theological translation and Bible study, who sometimes gets stuck on a question and takes it apart through the prism to look again.
